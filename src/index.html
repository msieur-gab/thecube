Who runs the cube?

I've been building with AI since 2011.

I've trained models and built the datasets behind them, and seen how a dataset's quality shapes what a model can do. I know why LLMs carry bias, and that they are one kind of AI among many, often not the right one.

So my first question is never which model. It's why you want AI, and what it changes for your company: where it becomes leverage for your people to grow the business. The core of your business isn't your technology. It's your people.

What happens when we work together?

One method, six faces.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Your brief describes what hurts. I listen for what it doesn't say.

  2. 02

    Question

    We look for the question underneath: why this, why now, and for whom.

  3. 03

    Prototype

    A first answer, built quickly, so we have something real to react to.

  4. 04

    Reframe

    The prototype shows what the brief left out. That is where the real problem appears.

  5. 05

    Abstain

    We decide what not to build, collect or automate. What remains is stronger for it.

  6. 06

    Hand over

    You keep an answer your own people can run, explain and grow.
